Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

PriceQuake ("we") runs an automated tool, "PriceQuake Uploader", that creates data-comparison videos and publishes them to the PriceQuake YouTube channel owned by us.

What the app accesses

The app uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos to our own channel and to read basic public statistics (such as view counts) of our own videos, so we can learn which topics perform best. It is used only with our own Google account.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data of viewers or any other users. We do not use cookies or tracking on this website.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By watching our videos you are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access granted to the app can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.
